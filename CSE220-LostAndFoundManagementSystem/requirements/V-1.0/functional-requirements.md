## Functional Requirements

| ID | Requirement | Priority | Sprint | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| FR-01 | The system shall enable user to register and login to his/her account. | High | SP-1 | Planned/Implemented |
| FR-02 | The system shall be capable of reporting a lost item with the necessary item details. | High | SP-1 | Planned/Implemented |
| FR-03 | The system shall enable a found item to be reported by entering details of the found item. | High | SP-1 | Planned/Implemented |
| FR-04 | The system shall enable people to search for lost or found items by entering keywords. | High | SP-1 | Planned/Implemented |
| FR-05 | The system shall provide filtering options to narrow search results according to category and location information. | Medium | SP-1 | Planned/Implemented |
| FR-06 | The system shall enable users to view detailed information about lost and found items. | Medium | SP-1 | Planned/Implemented |
| FR-07 | The system shall enable users to file a claim for a found item. | High | SP-2 | Planned/Implemented |
| FR-08 | The system shall enable the user to present proof of ownership when claiming an item. | High | SP-2 | Planned/Implemented |
| FR-09 | The system shall enable authorized personnel to validate submitted item claims. | High | SP-2 | Planned/Implemented |
| FR-10 | The system shall enable authorized staff to approve or reject claims based on ownership verification. | High | SP-2 | Planned/Implemented |
| FR-11 | The system shall change an item's status after a claim is approved or rejected. | High | SP-2 | Planned/Implemented |
| FR-12 | The system shall inform users of updates to report, claim, and verification status. | Medium | SP-2 | Planned/Implemented |
| FR-13 | The system shall provide administrators with control over user and item records. | Medium | SP-2 | Planned/Implemented |
| FR-14 | The system shall enable administrators to generate reports of lost and found activities. | Medium | SP-2 | Planned/Implemented |

## Traceability Summary

Sprint 1 covers account access, reporting, searching, filtering, and item details. Sprint 2 covers claims, ownership verification, notifications, administrative management, status changes, and reporting. These requirements should be linked to the project's user stories, UML designs, and test cases.
