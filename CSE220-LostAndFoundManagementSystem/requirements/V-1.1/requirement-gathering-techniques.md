## Requirement Gathering Techniques Used

### 1. Team Discussion and Brainstorming
The project team discussed the main problems and required functions of the Lost and Found Management System. This helped identify requirements related to registration, reporting lost and found items, searching, claims, notifications, and administration.

### 2. Document Analysis
The team reviewed the project instructions, user stories, product backlog, and existing project documentation to identify functional and non-functional requirements.

### 3. Backlog Refinement
The team reviewed and refined user stories during sprint planning. Requirements were prioritized using MoSCoW and estimated using Fibonacci story points.

### 4. Sprint Review and Team Feedback
Requirements were reviewed during Sprint 1 and Sprint 2 activities. Team feedback was used to clarify and improve the project requirements.
